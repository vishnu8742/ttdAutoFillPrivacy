How it works

Two minutes to set up. Once.

1 Settings

Tell it your family, one time

Each person’s full name, age, gender, photo ID type and number — plus the contact block TTD asks for on every booking: email, mobile, gothram, state, city, pincode and address. You will not type any of it again.

2 Before you book

Tick who is travelling

Open the extension and select the people on this trip — up to six, which is TTD’s own limit per booking — then go through to the slot-booking page as you normally would.

Only the people you tick are ever filled in.

3 On the form

One click fills every pilgrim

An Auto Fill button appears on the slot-booking step and nowhere else. Press it, and every pilgrim row and the whole contact section are written out in order.

Need only the contact block? General only does that instead.

Then you read it over, solve the captcha, pay, and press submit — yourself.

The extension stops the moment the form is filled. It never submits, never books, never pays and never touches a captcha. It gives you no advantage in getting a slot — it only spares you the typing.
Your data

Nothing leaves your browser.

Aadhaar numbers and family details are not ours to hold, so we never take them. There is no server to send them to.

No server, no account

Nothing to sign up for, nothing to sign in to. The extension makes no network requests at all.

Kept on your machine

Details live in your browser’s own private extension storage. Not synced, and not readable by any website.

No analytics, no ads

No telemetry, no tracking, no third-party scripts. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone, ever.

Erased in one click

Settings → Erase everything wipes it immediately. Uninstalling does the same thing.

Please read this bit Aadhaar and passport numbers are sensitive. They are stored unencrypted in your browser profile, much as saved form data is, so anyone with access to your computer and that profile could read them. On a shared machine, use a separate browser profile. And if you save a relative’s details, please do so with their agreement.

Questions

Anything else, write to me — I answer.

Is this an official TTD app?

No. It is an independent tool built by one person. Tirumala Tirupati Devasthanams has not authorised or endorsed it, and it has no connection to TTD. Always book through TTD’s own website.

Does it help me get a slot, or book faster than others?

No. It does not refresh, queue, retry or automate anything. It types details you already saved into a form you already opened. The only thing it saves is your typing.

Where exactly is my Aadhaar number kept?

In your browser’s private extension storage on your own computer — not on any server, because there is no server. It is not encrypted, so treat it the way you would treat details saved in your browser’s autofill.

Can I use it on my phone?

It is a desktop browser extension, so it runs in Chrome, Edge, Brave and Firefox on a computer. Mobile browsers do not support extensions in the same way.

Why does it need access to the TTD website?

To read the form’s fields and type into them. That access is limited to TTD’s booking domains — it asks for nothing on any other site.

Is it really free?

Yes. After a booking is confirmed it may once show a UPI QR code inviting a voluntary tip. Nothing is charged, no payment passes through the extension, and “Don’t ask again” stops it for good.

A field did not fill in. What now?

TTD updates its booking form from time to time, which can move or rename fields. Fill the missing ones by hand for now, and please email me what you saw — that is how this gets fixed.
