Stop retyping your family’s details on every TTD booking.

A browser extension that remembers who travels with you — names, ages, photo IDs, contact details — and fills the TTD darshan booking form in one click.

Add to Chrome Add to Firefox

Free, no account, no ads. Works on desktop Chrome, Edge, Brave and Firefox.

Not affiliated with, authorised by, or endorsed by Tirumala Tirupati Devasthanams. This extension only types into the booking form on your behalf. It never submits the form, never books, never pays, and never touches captchas. It gives you no advantage in obtaining a slot — it only saves you the typing.

How to use it

Roughly two minutes to set up, once.

  1. Install the extension

    Add it from the Chrome Web Store or Firefox Add-ons, then pin the icon to your toolbar so it is easy to reach.

  2. Save your family, once

    Open the extension and go to Settings. Add each person’s full name, age, gender, photo ID type (Aadhaar or Passport) and ID number. Fill in the contact block too — email, mobile, gothram, country, state, city, pincode and address.

  3. Start your booking on the TTD site as usual

    Log in at the official TTD portal, choose your darshan and date, and go through to the pilgrim details step. The extension only wakes up on TTD’s booking pages.

  4. Tick who is travelling

    Open the extension and select the people on this trip — up to six, which is TTD’s own limit per booking.

  5. Click Fill

    Every pilgrim row and the contact section populate instantly.

  6. Check it, then submit it yourself

    Read every field before you continue. You solve the captcha, you make the payment, you press submit. The extension stops the moment the form is filled.

Where your data goes

Nowhere. That is the whole design.

No server, no account

There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to sign in to. The extension makes no network requests at all.

Stored on your device

Details live in your browser’s own private extension storage, on your computer. They are not synced and not readable by websites.

No analytics, no ads

No telemetry, no tracking, no third-party scripts, and nothing is ever sold or shared with anyone.

Delete it in one click

Settings → Erase everything wipes it immediately. Uninstalling does the same.

Aadhaar and passport numbers are sensitive. They are stored unencrypted in your browser profile, much like saved form data, so anyone with access to your computer and profile could read them. On a shared machine, use a separate browser profile. The full detail is in the privacy policy.

Questions

 

Is this an official TTD app?

No. It is an independent, unofficial tool built by one person. Tirumala Tirupati Devasthanams has not authorised or endorsed it, and it has no connection to TTD. Always book through TTD’s own website.

Does it help me get a slot, or book faster than others?

No. It does not refresh, queue, retry, or automate the booking in any way. It types details you already saved into a form you already opened. The only thing it saves you is typing.

Where exactly is my Aadhaar number kept?

In your browser’s private extension storage on your own computer — not on any server, because there is no server. It is not encrypted, so treat it the way you would treat details saved in your browser’s autofill.

Can I use it on my phone?

It is a desktop browser extension, so it runs in Chrome, Edge, Brave and Firefox on a computer. Mobile browsers do not support extensions in the same way.

Why does it ask for access to the TTD website?

To read the form’s fields and type into them. That access is limited to TTD’s booking domains — it requests nothing for any other site.

Is it really free?

Yes. After a booking is confirmed, it may once show a UPI QR code inviting a voluntary tip. Nothing is charged, no payment passes through the extension, and “Don’t ask again” stops it permanently.

A field did not fill in. What now?

TTD updates its booking form from time to time, which can move or rename fields. Fill the missing ones by hand for now, and please email me what you saw — that is how the extension gets fixed.
