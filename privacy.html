Privacy Policy

Effective 8 September 2026 · applies to the browser extension “Auto Fill for TTD Bookings (Unofficial)” for Chrome, Edge and Firefox.

Not affiliated with, authorised by, or endorsed by Tirumala Tirupati Devasthanams.

The short version

The developer collects nothing. There is no server, no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party service of any kind. Everything you type stays in your own browser profile on your own device.

What the extension stores

Only what you enter yourself, in the extension’s Settings page:

DataWhy it exists
Full name, age and gender of each family memberTo fill the pilgrim rows of the booking form
Photo ID type (Aadhaar or Passport) and ID numberThe booking form requires them
Email, mobile number, gothram, country, state, city, pincode, addressThe contact section of the booking form
Which people are on the current booking, and a few display preferencesSo the extension knows whom to fill
Whether you dismissed the support prompt, and a booking reference used only to avoid showing it twiceSo it does not nag you

Sensitive data This includes government identity numbers (Aadhaar / passport). Please read “Where it is stored” below before entering them.

Where it is stored

In chrome.storage.local — the extension’s private storage area inside your browser profile, on your computer. It is:

  • not encrypted. It sits in your browser profile like saved form data. Anyone with access to your computer and your browser profile can read it. If you share a machine, use a separate browser profile.
  • not synced. It does not travel to your other devices or to any account.
  • not readable by websites, including TTD’s, and not readable by other extensions.

What is never done

  • No data is transmitted anywhere. The extension makes no network requests at all — it has no server to talk to.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, fingerprinting or usage tracking.
  • No selling, sharing or transfer of data to anyone, for any purpose.
  • No remote code. All code ships inside the extension package; nothing is fetched or evaluated at runtime.
  • Your booking itself is never read, stored or transmitted. On the confirmation page the extension looks for a reference number only to avoid showing its support prompt twice for the same booking, and that reference stays on your device.

Permissions, and why each one exists

PermissionWhy
storageTo save the details above on your device.
clipboardWriteSo the “Copy” button on the support screen can copy a UPI ID.
Access to ttdevasthanams.ap.gov.in and tirupatibalaji.ap.gov.inThe extension only runs on TTD’s booking site, to read the form’s fields and type into them. It requests no access to any other website.

The support prompt

After a booking is confirmed, and from the extension menu, the extension can show a UPI QR code inviting a voluntary tip to the developer. The QR image is generated on your device. No payment is processed by the extension, and it never sees whether, or how much, you paid. “Don’t ask again” stops it permanently.

Deleting your data

  • Settings → Erase everything removes all saved data immediately.
  • Uninstalling the extension deletes its storage along with it.
  • Because nothing is ever sent anywhere, there is nothing to request the deletion of from the developer — there is no copy to delete.

Other people’s data

If you save details for family members, you are entering another person’s identity information. Please do so only with their agreement, and take particular care with details of children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this URL with a new effective date, and the change will be described in the extension’s store listing release notes.
